01 / THE BASICS

🧪 What are pesticides?

Pesticides are substances used to control organisms that can damage crops or cause other problems. They need careful handling because some can be harmful if people or the environment are exposed unnecessarily.

🌾 Why are they used?

01To help protect crops from harmful pests.
02To reduce damage caused by insects, weeds and other pests.
03To support crop production when used responsibly.

⚠️ Why does safety matter?

01People should avoid unnecessary contact with pesticide products.
02Animals and water sources should be protected from contamination.
03Labels, protective equipment and secure storage are important safety barriers.
⚠️

THE GOLDEN RULE

Always read and follow the product label and applicable safety instructions. Never assume that every pesticide has the same hazards or precautions.

03 / THE SAFETY CYCLE

🔁 Think before → during → after.

🧠

BEFORE

Read the label, understand the safety requirements and make sure the appropriate protective equipment is available.

🧤

DURING

Follow the product instructions and safety precautions. Avoid eating, drinking or smoking while working.

🧼

AFTER

Wash exposed skin, shower or bathe, change contaminated clothing and keep protective clothing separate.

04 / SAFE STORAGE

🔒 Lock it.
Store it smart.

Good storage is about preventing accidental access, leaks, damage and contamination.

☠️ CAUTION • AUTHORIZED ACCESS
🧴📦🧴📦
📦🧴📦🧴
🔒
01

🚫 Keep Away

Store pesticides away from people, animals and water sources.

02

🔐 Authorized Access

Keep them in a high, secure or fenced area accessible only to authorized persons.

03

☀️💧 Protect From Weather

Protect pesticides from sunlight, water and moisture.

04

🌬️ Ventilation

The storage room should be secure and well ventilated.

05

📦 Stack Safely

Stack containers, bags and boxes safely. Follow “first expiry, first out”.

06

📋 Keep Records

Keep stock and issue registers updated and allow access only to authorized personnel.

07

☠️ Warning Sign

Display a caution or poison sign and keep the store locked.

08

🧱 Prevent Damage

Arrange containers to prevent damage, leakage and falling. Stack safely at a proper height.
